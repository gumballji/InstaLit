Comparison board

Satan vs Robinson Crusoe

A clear board for similarities, differences and the strongest literary argument.

Satan

Core energy: pride, rebellion and ambition.

Relationship with God: open opposition. He turns freedom into disobedience and revenge.

Social-media identity: dramatic influencer, rebel leader, master of persuasion.

Similarities

Both characters are isolated, want control, create their own little kingdoms and use language to justify their choices.

Both profiles show the tension between freedom and responsibility.

Robinson Crusoe

Core energy: survival, practicality and self-reliance.

Relationship with God: he gradually reads his survival as Providence and moral education.

Social-media identity: survival vlogger, island planner, practical life coach.

Strong final argument

Satan and Crusoe both want independence, but their independence has different meanings. Satan's freedom is destructive because it is ruled by pride; Crusoe's independence becomes constructive because it is linked to work, discipline and religious reflection. This is why the two profiles look similar as social-media creators, but morally they move in opposite directions.
